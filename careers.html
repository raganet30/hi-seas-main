Administrative Assistant
Job Description

Provides administrative and clerical support to ensure efficient operation of the office, including document management, scheduling, and coordination.

Qualifications
  • Bachelor’s degree in Office Administration or related field
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office applications
  • Strong organizational and communication skills
  • Experience in an office setting preferred
Responsibilities
  • Prepare and manage correspondence, reports, and documents
  • Organize meetings, appointments, and admin tasks
  • Maintain filing systems and record-keeping
Marketing Associate
Job Description

Supports marketing initiatives through research, social media management, and event coordination to strengthen brand awareness.

Qualifications
  • Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business, or related field
  • Strong understanding of digital and social media trends
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
Responsibilities
  • Assist in campaign creation and execution
  • Coordinate promotional events and materials
  • Analyze market data and report insights
Electrician
Job Description

Installs, maintains, and repairs electrical systems and equipment to ensure operational safety and efficiency.

Qualifications
  • Vocational course or certification in Electrical Installation
  • TESDA certification is an advantage
  • Knowledge of electrical safety codes
Responsibilities
  • Perform electrical wiring and maintenance
  • Troubleshoot system issues and perform repairs
  • Ensure compliance with safety standards
Accounting Associate
Job Description

Handles accounting tasks including bookkeeping, report generation, and assisting in financial audits.

Qualifications
  • Bachelor’s degree in Accountancy, Finance, or related field
  • Proficiency in accounting software and MS Excel
  • Strong attention to detail
Responsibilities
  • Maintain accurate financial records
  • Assist in preparing financial statements
  • Support month-end and year-end closing processes
