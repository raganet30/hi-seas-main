    Our Company

    Founded in 2019, HI-SEAS CO. LTD. INC. began with a strong foundation in telecommunications and engineering, evolving into a driver of digital innovation. At the core of our work is HiTok , an integrated messaging and services platform that simplifies communication and collaboration. Through our Barangay Broadband Project , we bring both free and affordable internet access to underserved communities across the Philippines, supporting digital inclusion and local growth. Backed by industry accreditations, we remain committed to building reliable technologies that connect people, empower communities, and create opportunities for a more inclusive digital future.
